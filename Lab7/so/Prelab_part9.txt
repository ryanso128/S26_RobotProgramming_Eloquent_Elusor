This node functions as a ROS 2 differential-drive robot driver that connects velocity commands from ROS to the wheel motors in Webots. It creates a subscription to the cmd_vel topic using the geometry_msgs/msg/Twist message type. When a message is received, the node stores the requested motion and, during each step of execution, reads linear.x as the forward or backward speed and angular.z as the turning speed. It then uses differential-drive kinematics to calculate separate left and right wheel velocities and sends those values directly to the motors. As a result, the robot can move forward, backward, rotate in place, and follow curved or arcing paths while moving. Because only linear.x and angular.z are used, the robot cannot move sideways or perform motion in 3D space. The motors are placed in velocity-control mode by setting their position to infinity, meaning they spin continuously at the commanded speeds rather than moving to a fixed position. Overall, this node serves as the low-level controller that translates ROS movement commands into physical wheel motion for a two-wheeled mobile robot.
