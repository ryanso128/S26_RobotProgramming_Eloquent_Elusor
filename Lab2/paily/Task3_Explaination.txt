First I used "ros2 service call /reset std_srvs/srv/Empty" to clear the turtle sim window and reset the turtle's position. Then I used "ros2 service call /turtle1/set_pen turtlesim/srv/SetPen "{r: 0, g: 0, b: 0}" " to change the pen color to black. Next I ran "r os2 topic pub --once /turtle1/cmd_vel geometry_msgs/msg/Twist "{linear: {x: 2.0, y: 0.0, z: 0.0}, angular: {x: 0.0, y: 0.0, z: 0.0}}"" followed by "ros2 action send_goal /turtle1/rotate_absolute turtlesim/action/RotateAbsolute "{theta: 1.57}"" 4 times. These commands moved the turtle forward and then turned it 90 degrees to the left.
