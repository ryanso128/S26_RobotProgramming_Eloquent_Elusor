I used the RotateAbsolute action (syntax: ros2 action send-goal /turtle1/rotate_absolute turtlesim/action/RotateAbsolute) to rotate to the equivalents of 90, 180, and 270 degrees (except it was expressed in radians). In between each rotation, I used the arrow keys to move the turtle a consistent amount forward in the direction it was facing. This took advantage of the turtle_teleop_key publisher.
