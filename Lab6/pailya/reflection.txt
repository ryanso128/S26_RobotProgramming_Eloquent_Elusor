In assessing backgorund knowledge, we looked at 2 frames and decided how they are positioned in relation to each other. If Frame B is +1 in the x and +1 in the y relative to A, then B is [1,1] with respect to A. When we consider a 3d space we must consider the right hand rule to determine our axis. The thumb will point in the +z direction and the fingers will curve from the +x into the +y.
